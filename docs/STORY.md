# Story

## Arc

**Some stories are worth keeping.** Chloe receives an invitation from the Curator, keeper of an unfinished collection. It starts as a Christmas mystery. The records grow familiar: places, dates, a film, a windy beach, a wedding, a first home. Two Junior Investigators interrupt with pencilled notes. On Day 23 the evidence names the Curator: Dan. The investigators are Oliver and George. On Christmas Eve the boys' birth days (01 and 30, eldest first) make the Christmas Key. On Christmas morning the key opens the vault into the Christmas Room, a letter, and an invitation to open her real presents.

## Title

The invitation shows only "The 25th Key" and "An unfinished collection", so nothing gives the twist away before it is earned. The full title, "The 25th Key: The Story of Us", is served by the server only after the vault opens, and appears for the first time in the Christmas Room. The public build check blocks the phrase from ever reaching public files.

## Voices

- **The Curator** (Dan, revealed on Day 23): courteous, dry, slightly theatrical, often wrong about cake. Short sentences. Never gushing until the reveal.
- **The Junior Investigators** (Oliver and George, revealed on Day 20): a single pencilled note on Day 12 and a correction on Day 15. These are fictional, in-character lines for Dan to approve or replace. They are not presented as anything the boys actually said.
- **Case files** (Day 20): written in the third person from the canonical descriptions, so nothing is attributed to the boys. If Dan uploads a recording to the `investigators` slot, it plays above the case files, which remain as the text equivalent.

- Oliver born 1 December 2016: analytical, thoughtful, inquisitive; magic, Rubik's cubes, fidgets, puzzles. George born 30 September 2019: energetic, funny, imaginative; superheroes, Hot Wheels.
